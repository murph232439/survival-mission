A TEAM SURVIVAL CHALLENGE

365 DAYS

Location: Classified Remote Island

DAY 0: THE CRASH

Last night, a violent storm destroyed your ship.

You wake up in the mud. It is pitch black.

You light a purple distress flare. It reveals the beach...

The only other survivors are the people at your table.

THE HARSH REALITY

  • NO Supermarket, NO clean water.
  • NO Hospital, NO medicine.
  • NO Signal, NO electricity.
  • NO RESCUE. (ETA: 1 Year)

WHAT WILL KILL YOU FIRST?

The Rule of 3

3 Minutes without Oxygen

3 Hours without Shelter (in extreme weather)

3 Days without Water

3 Weeks without Food

THE HOPE

In the light of the flare, you see it.

A Military Emergency Drop Box.

It contains 16 survival tools. But the lock is damaged.

MISSION RULES

Constraint: Your team can only carry 6 items into the jungle.

Phase 1 (Private): Choose 1 item you personally want to protect.

Phase 2 (Debate): Argue with your team. Eliminate the weak choices.

Ultimate Rule: Everyone must speak. You must agree on exactly 6 items.

TACTICAL COMMUNICATION

Use the O.R.E.O. Framework

Opening Opinion: "I strongly believe we MUST keep the..."

Reason: "The main reason is that without it, we..."

Example: "For example, if it rains, this will help us to..."

Objection / Over to you: "I see your point, but [Item] is more urgent because..."
